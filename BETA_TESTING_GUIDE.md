# Approvl Beta Testing Guide

Welcome to the Approvl beta! Thank you for helping test the app. This guide will walk you through everything step by step.

## What is Approvl?

Approvl is an app for music producers and artists to share, review, and approve audio mixes. Producers can upload multiple versions of songs, and artists/approvers can listen, comment, and approve their favorites.

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Install TestFlight

1. Download **TestFlight** from the App Store (it's Apple's official beta testing app)
2. Open TestFlight on your device

### Step 2: Join the Beta

1. You'll receive an invitation email from TestFlight
2. Tap the link in the email or enter the code in TestFlight
3. Tap **Accept** to join the beta
4. Tap **Install** to download Approvl

---

## For Producers (Creating Projects)

### Your First Project

1. **Open Approvl** and create an account
   - Use your real email address
   - Choose "Producer/Engineer" as your role

2. **Create a New Project**
   - Tap the **+** button
   - Give your project a name (e.g., "My Album Demo")
   - Add a description (optional)

3. **Add a Song**
   - Inside your project, tap **Add Song**
   - Enter the song title
   - Choose a genre (optional)

4. **Upload a Mix**
   - Tap **Add Mix** on your song
   - Select an audio file from your device
   - Give it a version name (e.g., "Mix v1" or "Mastered")
   - Add notes about what's different in this version (optional)

5. **Invite an Approver**
   - Go to your project settings (gear icon)
   - Tap **Invite Reviewer**
   - Enter their email address
   - Choose if they're a "Key Approver" (has final say)
   - An invitation email will be sent automatically

---

## For Approvers/Artists (Reviewing Projects)

### Getting Started as an Approver

1. **Check Your Email**
   - You'll receive an invitation to review a project
   - The email will have instructions

2. **Install Approvl** (see TestFlight steps above)

3. **Create Your Account**
   - Use the **same email address** that received the invitation
   - The app will automatically detect you're an invited approver
   - You'll see: "You've been invited to: [Project Name]"
   - Your role will be set to Artist/Approver (free account)

4. **The Project Appears Automatically**
   - Once signed in, you'll see the project you were invited to
   - Tap to open it

### Reviewing Mixes

1. **Listen to a Mix**
   - Tap on a song to see its mixes
   - Tap on a mix to play it
   - Use the waveform to scrub through the audio
   - You can loop sections to focus on specific parts

2. **Add Comments**
   - Tap the **comment** button while listening
   - Record a voice note OR type text
   - Your comment is timestamped to where you were in the song
   - Tap **Save** to send it to the producer

3. **Approve a Mix**
   - When you're happy with a mix, tap the **checkmark** button
   - Your approval is recorded
   - The producer will see which mixes are approved

#### 1. Account Creation

- [ ] Add a text comment

- [ ] Are projects, songs, and mixes the same on both?
- [ ] Do comments appear on both devices?

## Common Issues & Solutions

### "I can't see the project I was invited to"
- Make sure you signed up with the **exact email** that received the invitation
- Try the manual sync button in Settings
- Sign out and sign back in

### "Audio won't play"
- Check your device volume
- Try a different mix
- Close and reopen the app
- Let us know the file format you uploaded

### "App crashed"
- Please note what you were doing when it crashed
- Reopen the app - your data should be saved
- Report the crash (see below)

### "Files won't upload"
- Check your internet connection
- Try a smaller file first
- Make sure the file is an audio format (MP3, WAV, M4A, etc.)

## How to Report Issues

When you find a bug or issue, please email: **support@studioguru.net**

### Include This Information:
1. **What you were doing** (e.g., "Uploading a mix")
2. **What happened** (e.g., "App crashed")
3. **What you expected** (e.g., "File should upload")
4. **Device info**: iPhone or iPad? Which model? iOS version?
5. **Screenshots** if possible (very helpful!)

### Example Bug Report:
```
Subject: Bug - Upload Failed

I tried to upload a WAV file (150MB) to my project "Demo Album"
but it got stuck at 50% and never finished. I was on WiFi.

Device: iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 18.2
Screenshot attached.
```

## Tips for Effective Testing

1. **Use Real Content**: Upload actual songs/mixes you're working on - this tests real-world usage
2. **Test Edge Cases**: Try uploading very large files, very long songs, lots of comments, etc.
3. **Think Like a User**: Pretend you've never seen the app before - is it intuitive?
4. **Test Collaboration**: Find another beta tester and work on a project together
5. **Multiple Devices**: If you have multiple devices, test syncing between them
